# Athar for parents

Athar keeps your child's results from year to year and shows you where they are doing well and where to help first. It runs on your own computer. Nothing is sent anywhere, there is no account with any company, and it works with no internet.

Arabic version: [FOR-PARENTS.ar.md](FOR-PARENTS.ar.md).

## The first five minutes

1. **Start Athar.** The first time, it asks who is using it. Choose **I am a parent** and type your name. Your name is only shown on your own computer, beside the notes you write.
2. **Add your children.** For each child, type a first name and choose the grade they are in now, then press **Add this child**. Add as many as you have. Each child gets their own page.
3. **Add a first result.** Press **Add a first result**, or **Add a result or a note** in the side panel.

Not ready to type? Press **Add two made-up children to look around**. They have three years of invented results, so every page has something to show. Remove them whenever you like.

## Your children

- The side panel lists your children. Click a name to see that child. **+ Add another child** is under the list.
- There is no password and no account for a child.
- A child moves up a grade by themselves each school year. You do not need to change anything.
- To change a name, go to **Family and settings**, edit it, and press **Save name**.
- Each child has a short code, such as R-04. Athar uses the code instead of the name when it reads a typed paper.

## Adding a result

A result is one test, exam or piece of homework. Athar needs to know what each part of it was about, because a total such as 14 out of 20 cannot show where the difficulty is.

**From a photo.** Lay the marked paper flat in good light, take the photo straight from above, and choose it under **A photo of a marked paper**. Athar reads the marks and fills in the lines. Then:

- check every line against the paper;
- choose the topic for any line that has none;
- tick **I checked every line against the paper**, and press **Save these results**.

Nothing is saved before you tick the box. Athar reads a clear, straight photo well. On a dim or tilted photo it makes mistakes, and it does not tell you when it is unsure, which is why the check is yours. The photo is not kept and never leaves your computer. Reading a photo needs the model (see the last section).

To see how it works with no paper at hand, press one of the made-up papers under the photo box.

**By typing.** Choose the subject, the grade the paper was in, the term and the school year. For each part of the paper, choose the topic and type the mark and what it was out of. Old papers from earlier years are worth adding: the further back the results go, the more Athar can tell.

## Notes

Under **A note in your own words**, write what you notice when you study together: what clicks, what does not, what helped. A note is kept exactly as you wrote it. Write about the work, not about your child's worth, and leave health and private family matters out.

## What the home page tells you

Once a child has about six marks, **My child** shows:

- **Going well:** the topics and areas with the best results.
- **Where to help first:** the lowest topics, each followed back to the earlier topic where the difficulty may have started. Start with the earliest one.
- **Coming up this year:** topics still ahead that build on one that is low.
- **Questions you could ask the teacher.**

With fewer than six marks it says there is too little to tell, and says nothing else.

Athar never compares your child with anyone. It reads only the results you entered, and the teacher knows things these results do not. If something here does not match what you see, trust what you see.

## Keeping it safe

- Everything is in one file on your computer. In **Family and settings**, press **Make a backup now** and save a copy to a USB stick now and then.
- The same page can save one child's whole record as a file, or delete it for good.
- There is no password. Anyone who uses your computer can open Athar. Use your computer's own user accounts if that matters to you.

## The model

Everything above works with no model, except reading a photo and the **Read a paper** page.

On Windows, the first time you start Athar it offers to download the model: two files, 6.1 GB in all. Press Enter and wait. You do this once. After that it works with no internet. If you said no, double-click **Download the model** in the Athar folder later.

If you are also a teacher, **Family and settings** has a button to switch to the school side. Your family's records stay where they are, and the school side never shows them.
